Permutations built from delta swaps.

A delta swap exchanges two sets of bits a fixed distance apart in three operations (t = (x ^ (x >> s)) & m; x ^ t ^ (t << s)). Every bit permutation is a composition of at most 2·log₂ BITS − 1 of them (a Beneš network); the ones that matter in practice (8×8 board transposes, flips and rotations for bitboards and bit matrices) are three each and are provided here for u64.
