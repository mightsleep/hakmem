The even bits and the odd bits, each compacted to the low half: (pext(0x55…), pext(0xAA…)), the Morton decode, and Hacker’s Delight’s outer unshuffle (7-2) with its halves in two words. BMI2: two PDEP; portable: two expands, which under this mask fold to the shift ladder already.
