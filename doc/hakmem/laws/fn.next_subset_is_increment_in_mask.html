pub fn next_subset_is_increment_in_mask<W: Word>(x: W, mask: W) -> bool
Expand description

The carry-rippler is + 1 in the compacted domain: the next subset of mask after x is expand(compact(x) + 1), and None exactly after the mask itself. Bits of x outside the mask are ignored.
