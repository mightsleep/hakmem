Everything most code needs, one use away.

The traits whose methods you call (Word, Bits, Words on slices, Lanes, the curves Curve2 and Curve3) and the types you name (Wide, the Morton and Hilbert keys, Dilated). Iterator and view types come back from methods and are not here; name them from their modules.
